## Chronology (brief version)

1. **Rat Trap — intelligent adaptive switching (2024)**  
   Physical system first (ESP32 microcontroller). Not an AI product.  
   Modular tunnels, comfort phase, capture phase, sequential doors, threshold-based reconfiguration of open/close gates.  
   Coding assistance used at the time: Microsoft Copilot and Anthropic tools; author was not a professional programmer.  
   **This is the origin of dynamic gating in the stack.**

2. **Heron’s fountain turbine** (late February – early March)

3. **Rotational energy system** (shortly after; March 31 timestamp referenced by author)

4. **Adam’s Harmonic Principle**  
   Named principle in this lineage. Harmonic / ratio organization as a unifying idea.  
   Author reports Facebook and YouTube disclosure around March–early April 2025.

5. **Triadic law** (−1 / 0 / +1)  
   Push / hold / pull — contract, equilibrium, expand.  
   Comfort / activation / reset cycles map to the earlier Rat Trap phases.

6. **Eddies / vorticity**  
   Flow structures as organizers (streams, fans, larger-scale analogy).

7. **Pressure differentials**  
   Intuition for gravity-like behavior and for “pressure” as a basis for gated intelligence (when to expand vs hold).

8. **Pulse Nine**  
   Runtime / control layer: pulsed work, coherence, gates, duty cycle.  
   Note: the bare phrase “Pulse Nine” / “Pulsenine” appears in unrelated commercial products; **this** Pulse Nine means the Ada40 control stack (PCI, ADR, triadic, Rat Trap mapping).

9. **Double-helix topology** (and related structural ideas)

Later systems work in this line includes **PCI (Pulse Coherence Index)**, **ADR (survivor-only hierarchical memory)**, short-lived agents, local-first term resolution, and chat/coding agent designs built on the same gate logic.
